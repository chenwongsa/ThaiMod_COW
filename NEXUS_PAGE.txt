Complete Thai translation of theHunter: Call of the Wild (22,000+ strings):
[list]
[*]All menus, HUD, settings, store and equipment screens
[*]All main & side missions and dialogue on every reserve
[*]Codex, animal descriptions, skills, perks, challenges
[*]Custom Thai font + Thai main-menu logo
[*]Terminology reviewed for hunting accuracy across the whole game

One-click installer (.exe) — automatically finds your game, backs up
original files, and can cleanly uninstall (or just Verify Files).

refuses to run on mismatched game versions, so it cannot corrupt your
install.
Works in multiplayer (client-side only). Save games are not touched.
